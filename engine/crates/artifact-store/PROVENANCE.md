## Not adapted from TypeScript

- The on-disk claim file `.artifact-claims.json`, the commit epoch and the `artifacts.commit` operation (store.rs) are new. They give the engine the cross-process protection of the TypeScript queue lock (`withArtifactQueue`) across the gap between `put` and the caller's commit; see the amendment to ADR 0091.
- The file identity and process liveness calls come from `hoi4-os`.
