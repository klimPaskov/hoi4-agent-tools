- Territory controls for redrawing borders, from the ZIN map lane's rev3 findings. `assign_territory` takes culture or people `groups` (`{ provinceId: group }`): a growth step across a group line costs `1 + groupPenalty` times more (default 2), and with `groupHard: true` growth never crosses one. In rev3, peoples' pockets were shared across those lines. A country with `fixed: true` owns exactly its seeds and forced land and never grows. In rev3, Kingson still grew from 122 to 142 provinces into its neighbours' free bands. A preview and a write add `map-territory-assignment.json`, the provinces of each owner and of each new or changed state; before, the only source was the 22 MB proposed map. A seed in water is refused with the nearest land province named (`details.nearestLandProvince`).
