- New `hoi4.common_write` (advanced profile): add, replace or remove one balance of power, AI strategy, AI strategy plan or on_action in the mod's own `common/` files, touching only that block, from raw script bodies that must parse and are written as given. It is one descriptor-driven engine operation (`common.block_write`), so a further kind is a descriptor, not new code. A balance of power whose ranges do not cover -1 to 1 once, or whose sides do not match, is refused; an unknown strategy type, a missing abort, or a missing localisation key, sprite, category or focus is a warning; an on_action name the documentation does not list (and that is not `on_daily_TAG`, `on_weekly_TAG` or `on_monthly_TAG`) is refused with the documented names. on_action blocks merge by key: add appends to the key's block, and `blockIndex` replaces or removes one child block.
