- A partial `hoi4.event_inspect` result now says what is missing (issue 66, ADR 0086). `EVENT_INSPECTED_PARTIAL` carries an inline information diagnostic: `EVENT_TRACE_TRUNCATED` when a trace stopped at its limits (raise `maxDepth` or `maxNodes`), `EVENT_SOURCES_SKIPPED` with the count of skipped files, `EVENT_HELPER_EXPANSION_INCOMPLETE`, or `EVENT_FOCUSED_ANALYSIS_DEFERRED` when a large workspace defers the workspace-wide helper projections and state links, so that `roots` and `state_flow` answers may miss callers or links that exist only inside scripted effects. Before, a partial result gave no reason.
