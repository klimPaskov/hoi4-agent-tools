- When an apply's automatic rollback is refused (`TRANSACTION_ROLLBACK_STALE`, for example because another writer changed a target meanwhile), the rewrite engine now removes the in-workspace `.backup` files of every target still at its written bytes; their before-bytes stay in the journal. Only the conflicting targets keep a backup, and the refusal names them (`retainedBackups`, `retainedBackupCount`). Before, every backup stayed in the mod tree.
- Pruning now reclaims an expired `failed` journal once the workspace holds all of its written bytes, and library callers can discard a failed journal with `TransactionManager.discardFailed`. Before, a failed journal counted against the journal quota for good. No MCP tool exposes the discard.
