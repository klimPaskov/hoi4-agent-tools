- Add natural lands to `hoi4.map_inspect` (`lands: true`, thresholds in `landOptions`). Redrawing country borders along natural lines needs the land cut into the pieces its ground suggests, which a map lane did by hand in scratch scripts. The inspection now cuts the land provinces along major rivers (`rivers.bmp` width index 6 and up on at least 40% of a border), crests (the mean height along a border at least 3.5 above the higher province) and changes of terrain class (forest, hills and mountain, marsh, desert, open). It cuts open country into drainage basins (coasts and lakes are sinks, small basins merge over their lowest pass, and `maxBasinProvinces` splits big ones at branch points of the drainage tree), and merges lands below `minProvinces` (6) across their weakest line. Impassable borders are hard lines: an `impassable` adjacency, the edge of an `impassable = yes` state, or (for callers with a barrier plane) a 255 wall. Their two sides are never in the same land and no merge crosses them. `data.lands` gives the count per kind (`basin`, `range`, `forest`, `marsh`, `desert`, `plains`) and the first 50 lands; `map-natural-lands.<revision>.json` holds every land with its provinces, terrain shares, coast and the river, crest, terrain, impassable and divide lengths of its lines to each neighbour. `assign_territory` accepts the lands as `landIds` in `forced`, `blocked`, `cores`, `claims` and `unowned`, and `{ land }` as a seed, cut with the operation's `landOptions`. The ranges of a geography model, moving uplands to the side they drain to, and closed basins (rings) are not covered yet.
