- `hoi4.map_inspect` and the other map tools no longer build the shared symbol index (maintainer decision M-8, step 4). The map model parses its own sources and never read it, yet every map call indexed every scanned file, its localisation included. Map scans now use `CoreEngine.scanFiles`, which shares the scan cache and admission limit and skips the index. On the benchmark machine (median of three cold runs), `{"query":"Brandenburg","lookupOnly":true}` on the game took 6.4 s instead of 11.4 s, an unknown-state lookup on Chaos Redux 3.0 s instead of 4.5 s, and a full inspection of the game map 22.5 s instead of 27.1 s. Answers are unchanged. The heightmap check of a full inspection also stops its land-distance search one step past the three-pixel lake-bank allowance instead of flooding every sea pixel, with the same answers; on the game a full inspection took 13.7 s instead of 26.8 s. The new benchmark case `vanilla.map.full-inspect` measures it.
