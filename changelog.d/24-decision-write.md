- New tool `hoi4.decision_write` (issue 159, second half): add, replace or remove one decision of a mod `common/decisions/*.txt` file, touching only that block. Other decisions, their category blocks, comments, newline style and encoding come back as they were, and the result says how many other decisions were checked unchanged. `add` needs `category` (the block the decision goes in) and `body` (the text between the braces) and goes at the end of that block; `createCategory: true` writes a new category block at the end of the file, and `file` with `createFile: true` makes a new file (never one under `common/decisions/categories`). `replace` and `remove` find the decision by id in the mod, and moving a decision to another category is refused. It warns, and still writes, when the decision's name key has no English text, its `icon` sprite is not defined, or no category of that name is defined in `common/decisions/categories`. A game-only decision, a duplicate id, a body that is not one balanced block and a layout the edit cannot place safely are refused with the reason.
