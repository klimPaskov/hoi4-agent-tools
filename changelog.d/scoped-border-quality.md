- Measure border quality in one region. `hoi4.map_inspect` takes `qualityScope` with `quality`, and `assign_territory` and `repartition_states` take `borderQualityScope`. Each has `owners`, `stateIds` and a pixel `box`: owners and states add provinces, and the box cuts. A border pixel pair, and the chance baseline, count when either side is inside, so a country is measured against its neighbours. Before, the measurement always covered the whole map, so a redrawn region could not be compared before and after; the ZIN map lane asked for this. The result gives `scopePixels`, and the territory note says `inside the scope`. A scope that selects nothing is `MAP_BORDER_QUALITY_UNAVAILABLE` with the reason `MAP_BORDER_QUALITY_SCOPE_EMPTY`, a blocker in `enforce`. Requests without a scope measure as before.
