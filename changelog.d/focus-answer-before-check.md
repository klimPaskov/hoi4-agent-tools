- `hoi4.focus_rewrite` takes an optional `answerBeforeCheck: true`. The call then answers as soon as the edit is durably written, with `data.afterEditCheck` naming the job whose final result, read with `hoi4.job_inspect`, is the after-edit check's outcome. A failing check still undoes the edit. A second such rewrite waits for the running check instead of failing with `TRANSACTION_LOCKED`. Calls without the option are unchanged (issue 137, ADR 0087).
