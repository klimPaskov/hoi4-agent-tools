- GUI previews draw an `iconType` at its sprite's size, as the game does (issue 220). The game refuses `size` on an `iconType` ("Unexpected token: size" in `error.log`) and draws the sprite at its own size, but `hoi4.gui_render` and `hoi4.gui_inspect` stretched it to the refused `size`, so a window looked right offline and different in game. The scene now ignores that `size`, records it under the fidelity report's ignored fields, and draws a plain sprite at its texture's frame size and a `corneredTileSpriteType` at the size its `.gfx` declares (also when no `size` was written, where the texture size was used before). Other element types still take `size`, and parts the renderer builds itself (a scrollbar's stretched track, the tooltip preview's background) keep their computed size. The project's synthetic acceptance window has such icons, so its render goldens changed.
